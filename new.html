Join The Movement
01. Who is David Maraga?

The former Chief Justice of Kenya who annulled a presidential election, digitized courts, expanded access to justice and authored over 1,250 judgments. He is running for President in 2027.

02. What does the campaign stand for?

Reset. Restore. Rebuild Kenya. Our five pillars cover education and youth, the economy, healthcare and social justice, Pan-Africanism, and accountability and the rule of law.

03. How can I stay updated on campaign news?

Subscribe to our emails for campaign updates, rally announcements and policy highlights delivered straight to your inbox.

04. How can I get involved?

Join the movement as a volunteer — add your voice, skills and time to a people-powered campaign working to reset, restore and rebuild our country.

05. How do I register to vote?

Jiandikishe Kupiga Kura — use the voter registration drive on our site to find out how and where to register before 2027.

06. How are donations used?

Contributions fund outreach, events and voter mobilisation across all 47 counties as we build towards 2027.
